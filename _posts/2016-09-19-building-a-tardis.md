I've watched Doctor Who since I was a kid and it was on PBS in the US. I've introduced my kids to the fandom, and I've kept up with the modern incarnations of the show and its spin-offs.

<!--more-->

A while ago, I saw a post on the internet where someone built a TARDIS Murphy Bed. Being a big kid, and having Whovian kids of my own, I decided that I would try to do the same.

I did a lot of research into buying and building Murphy Bed kits, and assembling one seemed to be a pretty big project. Also, I have four left thumbs when it comes to building things. My attempts at being crafty are laughable. I also really wanted people to be able to sleep in the bed unharmed.

So, I came up with a scope of work and put out a few bids. Adam at Seattle Wall Beds was excited to take on the project, and we started to talk about what we could do.

I have to say, working with Adam was great. He has an attention to detail and went way above and beyond for us. He truly is an artist.

Adam and I chatted several times about my vision, and I pointed him to the article. I expected him to build a pretty standard bed, and paint it blue.

Well, as you can see, that's not at all what happened!

![The completed TARDIS Murphy Bed, closed and standing against the wall](/binaries/2016/09/tardis-completed.jpg)
<sub><i>The Completed Bed</i></sub>

It's a queen-sized bed, so the scale is just a little off, but since the Doctor parked it in the side of a wall, we're okay with that…

![The TARDIS bed nestled into the corner of the room](/binaries/2016/09/tardis-bad-parking.jpg)
<sub><i>Another bad parking job for the Doctor</i></sub>

The detail is just amazing, even down to the choices of wood and paint. Adam used Red Oak, and the grain is beautiful. The decals are all custom-made and sized for the bed. The paint is a custom color.

![Close up of a custom St John Ambulance decal on the Red Oak grain](/binaries/2016/09/tardis-details.jpg)
<sub><i>Details are what make this project shine</i></sub>

But at the end of the day, the only thing that really matters is having a couple of happy kids with an amazing piece of art.
